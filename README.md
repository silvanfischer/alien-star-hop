# Alien Star Hop

A 3D browser game. Jim, a three-eyed green alien, is stranded on Pluto. Collect 5 stars on each planet to call your star fighter, fly it through the asteroid fields, and hop from planet to planet until you reach Earth.

- Every planet has its own monsters: ice yetis, space jellies, crystal bugs, ring blobs, storm clouds and rock crabs.
- Earth is home to friendly dinosaurs and a few grumpy T-rexes that roar when they spot Jim.
- Plays with keyboard or touch (joystick, FIRE and JUMP buttons on phones). On phones, choose Joystick or Tilt phone steering on the title or pause screen; tilt uses the accelerometer.

## Play

Play it online: https://silvanfischer.github.io/alien-star-hop/

Or open `index.html` in a browser. It is a single file and loads Three.js r128 from cdnjs.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W / S | Walk forward / back |
| A / D | Turn left / right |
| Space | Shoot (aims by itself; hold to keep firing). In the star fighter: fire lasers |
| Shift | Jump (land on a monster to stun it) |
| P | Pause |
| M | Mute |

Secret map: type `jim` on the title screen, tap the title five times, or tap the faint star in the corner of the title card to start on any planet.
